01 / Campaign DevelopmentCreator Marketing

Developing a creator mix strategy for professional imaging products

Analyze how consumers in the US and European markets move from product awareness and professional evaluation to purchase consideration for monitors, wireless video transmitters, and related filmmaking equipment. Translate this decision journey into a complementary creator strategy: use established KOLs to build category authority and professional trust, while engaging workflow-focused KOCs to validate real-world usability. Prioritize creators rooted in filmmaking, independent production, cinematography, behind-the-scenes production, and specialist equipment reviews. Evaluate the substance of their work through real shooting scenarios, hands-on testing, workflow compatibility, and competitor comparisons rather than relying on follower count alone.

02 / Research FrameworkCreator Intelligence

Building a quantitative creator evaluation model

Develop a weighted screening framework for film and video equipment creators across the US, UK, and Europe. Score audience-market alignment, content verticality, engagement quality, publishing consistency, past sponsorship performance, and brand safety. Validate target-market audience share, comment authenticity, average-view stability, and the performance of relevant equipment reviews and integrations. Flag high-follower/low-impact, anomalous, and audience-mismatched accounts before shortlisting.
